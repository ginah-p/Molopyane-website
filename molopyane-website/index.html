About Molopyane Surgery

Molopyane Surgery (Inc.) is a trusted and well-established general practice and surgical medical practice with over 20 years of dedicated service to patients.

Built on clinical excellence, compassion and integrity, the practice is committed to delivering high-quality healthcare.

By combining general medical care with surgical expertise, Molopyane Surgery prioritises patient well-being at every stage of care — from consultation to recovery.

Every patient is unique. Our commitment is to provide compassionate, individualised care through every stage of treatment.
